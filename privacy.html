Privacy Policy

This application is used solely by its owner to upload pre-produced video content to YouTube channels owned by the same person, via the YouTube Data API v3.

This app does not collect, store, process, or share any personal data belonging to end users or third parties. It has no public-facing users — only the developer/owner authenticates with it, using their own Google account, to manage their own YouTube channels.

Data accessed via the YouTube Data API (channel and video metadata) is used only to perform uploads and is not shared with any third party.
